Bundled fonts: provenance and licences
======================================

These SVG stroke fonts were copied unmodified from the Hershey Text extension shipped with
Inkscape 1.4.3 (share/inkscape/extensions/svg_fonts).

- The EMS* fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see OFL.txt and each
  font's own <metadata> for its copyright holder and source).
- The Hershey* fonts carry the notice below, reproduced from their <metadata> as the notice
  itself requires.

Originally prepared in 2011 and converted to SVG fonts
in 2019 by Windell H. Oskay, www.evilmadscientist.com

Contents adapted from emergent.unpythonic.net/software/hershey
